🎆 Fireworks Safety Guide 🎆

Learn how to enjoy fireworks responsibly and safely

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Every year, fireworks cause serious injuries. By following these safety guidelines, you and your loved ones can enjoy fireworks safely.

General Safety Rules

🔒
Always supervise children: Keep children under close supervision at all times. Never allow children to handle fireworks unsupervised.
👓
Wear protective gear: Wear safety glasses and keep a fire extinguisher nearby. Consider wearing closed-toe shoes and long sleeves.
🌡️
Check weather conditions: Avoid using fireworks on extremely windy, dry, or rainy days. Wind can cause unpredictable behavior.
💧
Have water ready: Keep a bucket of water, garden hose, or fire extinguisher nearby for emergencies.

Do's and Don'ts

✓ DO

  • Read all warning labels and instructions
  • Use fireworks in an open area away from buildings
  • Keep fireworks on a level surface
  • Light one firework at a time
  • Wait 15-20 minutes before reusing a dud
  • Dispose of used fireworks in water
  • Wear protective eyewear
  • Have an adult present at all times

✕ DON'T

  • Never use fireworks indoors
  • Don't allow children to use fireworks alone
  • Never relight a faulty or damaged firework
  • Don't throw fireworks at people or animals
  • Never carry fireworks in your pocket
  • Don't use fireworks when under the influence
  • Never aim fireworks at your face or body
  • Don't use fireworks in prohibited areas

Before You Light Fireworks

🏘️ Clear the Area

Make sure there are no buildings, trees, or combustible materials nearby. Keep spectators at least 25 feet away.

🧯 Prepare Safety Equipment

Have water, a fire extinguisher, and a first aid kit readily available. Test that equipment works before you start.

📋 Read Instructions

Carefully read all instructions and warning labels. Follow the manufacturer's directions exactly as written.

👥 Plan for Help

Have responsible adults present. Designate someone to watch for fires and be ready to call emergency services if needed.

Common Injuries and Prevention

✋
Hand and Finger Injuries: The most common injury. Keep hands at safe distance. Never hold or light fireworks in your hand.
👁️
Eye Injuries: Can cause permanent blindness. Always wear safety glasses and never look directly at fireworks.
👂
Hearing Damage: Fireworks produce loud noise. Wear hearing protection or move further away for large fireworks.
🔥
Burn Injuries: Keep children away from hot fireworks. Never touch fireworks after they've been lit, even if they appear inactive.

What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

🚑
Call Emergency Services: If someone is injured, call 911 immediately. Don't wait or try to treat serious injuries at home.
💧
Minor Burns: Run cool (not cold) water on the burn for 10-20 minutes. Cover with a clean cloth and seek medical attention.
🔥
Fire Starting: If a fire starts, immediately evacuate the area and call emergency services. Don't attempt to fight a large fire.
👁️
Eye Injuries: Seek immediate medical attention. Rinse gently with clean water if something gets in the eye.

Firework Disposal

🌊 Cool Completely

Soak all used and unused fireworks in water for at least 24 hours before disposal to ensure they're completely extinguished.

🗑️ Dispose Properly

Seal in a plastic bag and dispose of in your regular trash. Do not throw hot fireworks in trash cans with other combustibles.

✓ Verify Safe

Make sure no fuses remain lit. Check that all fireworks are cool to touch before handling or disposing.

🚨 Emergency! Call for Help Immediately
