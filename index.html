Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
To place an order, simply browse our Shop page, add your desired fireworks to the cart, and proceed to checkout. Make sure you have enough credit balance to complete the purchase.
How do I track my order?
You can track your order in the Orders page after logging into your account. Order status is updated in real-time and you'll see if it's Pending, Approved, Completed, or Cancelled.
Do you offer meet-up options?
Yes! We offer meet-up options for customers within Calapan City and nearby areas. Coordinate with our support team first before placing order.
Is there a minimum order for free delivery?
Yes, we offer free delivery on all orders ₱1,999 and above around Calapan City. For orders below this amount, a standard delivery fee applies.
How can I add credits to my account?
You can recharge your balance by clicking the wallet icon next to your profile. Choose either GCash or Cash payment method, follow the instructions, and submit your recharge request. Admin will approve it within 1 hour.
How long does it take for my recharge to reflect?
Recharge requests are processed within 1 hour during business hours. Once approved by admin, the amount will be added to your balance automatically. If it doesn't reflect, try logging out and logging back in.
What payment methods are accepted?
We currently accept: GCash (via QR code or GCash number) & Cash (at our physical store in Calapan City)
Can I use my credits to pay for orders?
Yes! Your credit balance can be used to pay for your orders. During checkout, the system will automatically apply your available credits. If your credits are insufficient, you'll need to recharge first.
What is the GCash number for payment?
Our GCash number is 0963 386 3860 under the name JE*****L C.
Are your fireworks safe and certified?
Yes! All our fireworks are DTI-certified and undergo strict quality control. We ensure that every product meets safety standards before being sold.
Do you provide safety instructions?
Yes, every product comes with safety instructions. We strongly recommend following all safety guidelines when handling fireworks and keeping them away from children.
Can I return or exchange fireworks?
Due to safety regulations, we do not accept returns or exchanges once fireworks have left our store.
Do you offer bulk discounts?
Yes! We offer special wholesale pricing. Please contact our support team or visit our store for inquiries.
How do I create an account?
Click the profile icon, select "Create Account", fill in your full name, phone number, and password. You'll receive a unique Account ID after successful registration.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Currently, please contact our support team for password recovery. We're working on an automated password reset feature.
How do I redeem promo codes?
Go to the Featured page, enter your code in the Code Redemption section, and click Redeem. Your credits will be added instantly if the code is valid.
What is my Account ID for?
Your Account ID is a unique 9-digit number used to identify your account. It's required for admin assistance.
How do I contact customer support?
You can reach us through: Live Chat: Available on our website & Email: jlfworks.official@gmail.com
What are your support hours?
We're available 24/7 through our live chat and email support. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Where is your physical store located?
Our store is located at Brgy. Camansihan, Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro. Store hours are 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily.
How do I become a reseller?
We welcome resellers! Please contact our support team for reseller inquiries and wholesale pricing.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes, we take your privacy seriously. All personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your data with third parties.
Still Have Questions?
If your question wasn't answered here, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team. We're always happy to help!
