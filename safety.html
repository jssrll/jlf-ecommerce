Firework Safety Guide

Enjoy fireworks responsibly. Follow these essential safety guidelines for a memorable and safe celebration.

Before Lighting

Proper preparation is key to a safe fireworks display:

  • Read all labels and instructions carefully
  • Keep a bucket of water or hose nearby
  • Wear safety glasses when handling fireworks
  • Ensure adequate spacing from buildings and people
  • Check wind direction before lighting

During Display

Follow these rules while lighting fireworks:

  • Light one firework at a time
  • Never relight a "dud" firework - wait 20 minutes
  • Stand back immediately after lighting
  • Keep spectators at a safe distance
  • Never point fireworks at people or animals

After Display

Proper disposal ensures safety:

  • Wait 20 minutes after display to approach
  • Soak used fireworks in water before disposal
  • Check area for unexploded fireworks
  • Dispose in metal trash can if possible
  • Never put fireworks in regular trash immediately

Golden Rules of Firework Safety

👤 Adult Supervision Required

Children should never handle fireworks. Always have a responsible adult present during any fireworks activity.

🍺 No Alcohol or Drugs

Never use fireworks while under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Stay alert and focused.

🏠 Safe Distance

Maintain a minimum safe distance of 50 feet from spectators and 100 feet from buildings.

📱 Emergency Ready

Keep your phone nearby with emergency numbers ready. Know the location of the nearest hospital.

DO's

  • Buy fireworks from licensed dealers only
  • Use fireworks in open, clear areas
  • Keep water source readily available
  • Wear protective eyewear
  • Light fireworks on a flat, stable surface
  • Keep unused fireworks away from ignition source
  • Supervise pets and keep them indoors

DON'Ts

  • Don't point fireworks at anyone
  • Don't carry fireworks in pockets
  • Don't shoot fireworks from metal or glass containers
  • Don't experiment with homemade fireworks
  • Don't relight malfunctioning fireworks
  • Don't use fireworks near dry grass or vegetation
  • Don't give sparklers to young children

Sparkler Safety Tips

Sparklers burn at very high temperatures (up to 2,000°F) and require special attention:

  • Always wear gloves when holding sparklers
  • Hold sparklers at arm's length away from body
  • Never wave sparklers near others
  • Drop used sparklers in a bucket of water immediately
  • Children under 12 should not use sparklers without direct supervision
  • Consider glow sticks as a safer alternative for young children

In Case of Emergency

If an injury occurs, seek medical attention immediately. For fire-related emergencies, call your local fire department.

Emergency: 911
Fire Dept: Local Number
